OEM

1. CCC calibration guidelines now live in estimates

CCC launched recommendations from five leading calibration providers directly into CCC ONE and insurer estimating workflows, no extra charge. This means calibration flags will show up where estimates live, not buried in a separate system. Shops can cite OEM-backed guidance when pushback comes from carriers. Estimators can front-load the work upfront.

Mark says: Pull a sample estimate today and show your service writers where the recommendations appear; prep your pushback language now.

RECALL

2. Ford recalls 102,000+ F-Series trucks over faulty speedometer

Ford issued a safety recall for over 102,000 F-Series trucks due to faulty speedometer displays. NHTSA flagged the defect this week. Speedometer failure affects driver visibility of speed and potential ADAS function on newer models. Add this to your intake walkthrough this week.

Mark says: Put Ford F-Series recall lookup on your check-in iPad; flag any 2020+ models with speedometer complaints before they leave the lot.

Source: Reuters →

RECALL

3. Toyota recalls 105,800 US vehicles over fire risk

Toyota issued a safety recall affecting approximately 105,800 US vehicles over a fire risk condition. NHTSA confirmed the recall this week. Fire-related defects can ground vehicles and trigger multiple system inspections post-repair. Flag on intake.

Mark says: Cross-reference incoming Toyotas against recall list; fire-risk repairs often need wider inspection scope and documentation.

Source: Reuters →

OEM

4. Autel gains access to Nissan CONSULT 4 for ADAS calibration

Autel software now accesses Nissan's restricted CONSULT 4 vehicle line for ADAS calibrations and related services. CONSULT 4 normally locks down critical vehicle systems, but Autel's update cracks the door open for calibration work. Shops running Autel gear can now keep Nissan ADAS jobs in-house without workarounds.

Mark says: If you own Autel and have been outsourcing Nissan ADAS, update your software and recalculate labor savings on your next quote.

TRAINING

5. SCRS ADAS showcase expands at SEMA with live calibration bays

SCRS launched an ADAS Council and partnered with SEMA to expand the 2026 show floor showcase (Nov. 3-6, Upper South Hall). Two live calibration bays and daily OEM-led sessions with GM, Ford, and Rivian engineers will run throughout the show. This is hands-on training and relationship-building with the automakers in real time.

Mark says: If SEMA is on your calendar, block Nov. 3-6 Upper South Hall time; walking away with an OEM contact is worth the trip.
