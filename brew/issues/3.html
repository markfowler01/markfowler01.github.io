ADAS gets serious; parts pricing diverges
Monday, May 11 · #3

The collision industry is doubling down on ADAS—two new initiatives just launched to tackle the risks and opportunities of advanced driver assistance systems. Meanwhile, OEM parts are climbing while aftermarket stays flat, and LKQ's aftermarket utilization hit a record 40% in Q1.

TRAINING

1. Two new ADAS initiatives launch across industry

SCRS and the Collision Industry Conference just kicked off coordinated efforts to address ADAS-related risks and opportunities. The goal: create industry-wide best practices and community standards similar to what SCRS already offers for collision repair. Translation—ADAS is now officially too big for shops to wing it alone.

Source: SCRS →

INSURANCE

2. OEM parts prices surge; aftermarket holds steady

PartsTrader's Spring 2026 report shows OEM new parts seeing the largest price jump, with recycled parts climbing close behind—but aftermarket parts remain flat. This pricing divergence matters for your estimates and margins as shops lean harder on alternatives.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

INDUSTRY

3. LKQ hits record 40% alternative parts utilization

Alternative parts hit a record high of 40% in Q1 2026, signaling sustained demand for non-OEM components. This trend gives shops leverage in parts negotiations but also means staying current on compatibility and calibration requirements for ADAS-equipped vehicles.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

INDUSTRY

4. CCC profits up 12% as insurers embrace AI

CCC Intelligent Solutions posted $281.3M in Q1 revenue (up 12% YoY) on the back of insurers adopting AI-driven estimating and workflow tools. The shift is already reshaping how claims are scoped and approved—shops need to understand the algorithms now writing estimates.

Source: Collision Repair Magazine →

INDUSTRY

5. Driven Brands faces $18/share buyout bid from activist

ADW Capital Management is pushing to acquire Driven Brands for $18 per share in cash—a 41% premium over recent trading. If the deal closes, consolidation in the MSO space accelerates, potentially shifting how calibration work flows through the network.

Source: Repairer Driven News →
