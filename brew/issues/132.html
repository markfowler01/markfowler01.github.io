NHTSA is investigating 1.2 million Ford vehicles over airbag deployment issues. At the same time, Ford's latest technical bulletin covers SRS repair procedures your estimators need to reference on every supplement.

RECALL

1. NHTSA opens probe into 1.2M Ford vehicles over airbag deployment

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has opened an investigation into Ford vehicles suspected of improper airbag deployment. The probe covers 1.2 million units across multiple model years and platforms. No recall has been issued yet, but this signals a safety concern that will likely drive incoming claims and repair procedures.

Mark says: Add Ford airbag inspection to your intake checklist now; expect supplemental billings once recall scope clarifies.

TSB

2. Ford On Target covers Bronco SRS and supplemental restraint repair procedures

Ford's latest technical bulletin newsletter focuses on supplemental restraint system (SRS) repair procedures, including detailed guidance on Bronco SRS repowering. This is OEM-issued repair methodology directly from Ford that your service writers and estimators should cite when quoting SRS work. Tying estimates to Ford's bulletin strengthens your position on denials.

Mark says: Pull the Ford On Target bulletin and attach it to every SRS supplement submitted this month; reference it by issue number on every estimate.

INDUSTRY

3. What OSHA cites most in collision centers

OSHA's inspection data reveals the top safety violations cited at collision repair facilities. Common citations include improper lockout/tagout, personal protective equipment failures, and hazard communication gaps. Knowing where OSHA looks hardest protects your shop from fines and keeps your team safe during calibration and repair operations.

Mark says: Walk your calibration bays this week; check PPE compliance and lockout procedures before OSHA does the inspection for you.

Source: BodyShop Business →

INSURANCE

4. Jane Kim elected California Insurance Commissioner

Jane Kim has won the California Insurance Commissioner race after a close recount. Kim's election signals potential regulatory shifts in the state that oversees one of the largest insurance markets in North America. Collision repair billing, labor rate negotiations, and coverage disputes may face new policy direction in 2027.

Mark says: Monitor California insurance commissioner transition announcements; expect billing and labor policy guidance updates in Q1 2027.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

OEM

5. OEC launches AI repair planning tools and completes epyx acquisition

OEConnection has deployed RepairLogic, an AI-powered assistant that drafts OEM-sourced repair plans, and completed its acquisition of epyx, a fleet repair authorization network. The tools integrate parts search and repair planning into a single workflow. This consolidation signals faster estimate turnaround and tighter OEM data integration for shops using the platform.

Mark says: If you use OEC for estimates, review the new RepairLogic output and verify OEM repair plans match your calibration procedures before submitting.
