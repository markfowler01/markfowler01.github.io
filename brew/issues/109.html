TSB

1. Ford releases updated ADAS calibration Job Aid

Ford has released an updated ADAS Job Aid with refreshed component descriptions, locations, and calibration requirements across their lineup. This is the go-to cite when a Ford estimate hits your desk and the OEM procedure is questioned. Grab it and front-load your estimators before the weekend.

Mark says: Pull this bulletin and attach it to every Ford ADAS supplement you write this week to stop estimate denials.

RECALL

2. Toyota and Lexus steering knuckle recall issued

Toyota and Lexus have issued a recall affecting steering knuckles on certain model years. Vehicles with this defect face potential loss of power steering or steering control. Check your incoming repair queue for affected VINs and flag them for customer notification before any steering work begins.

Mark says: Flag Toyota and Lexus steering jobs; verify VIN against recall list before releasing any vehicle to the road.

Source: Autobody News →

TSB

3. Range Rover Sport subframe cracks emerging

Subframe cracking has been documented on certain Range Rover Sport models, creating a structural integrity issue that can affect alignment and suspension geometry. If you're seeing unexpected suspension complaints or alignment specs that won't hold on Range Rovers, subframe inspection is now a critical diagnostic step.

Mark says: Add Range Rover Sport subframe visual to your pre-alignment inspection until you know the affected years and VINs.

Source: Autobody News →

INSURANCE

4. Washington state fines USAA, Allstate $230K for claims handling violations

The Washington Insurance Commissioner levied $230,000 in fines against USAA and Allstate for unfair claims handling practices and other violations. This signals tighter state-level scrutiny of insurer behavior on supplement approvals and estimate handling. Keep records of denials and timeline issues; regulators are watching.

Mark says: Document every delayed supplement approval and denial; state regulators now have proof insurers are gaming the process.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

OEM

5. Hunter adds Bosch remote diagnostics to ADASLink scan tool

Hunter Engineering has integrated Bosch Remote Diagnostics Service into its ADASLink calibration platform, letting technicians tap Bosch diagnostic experts on demand. This bridges gaps on complex ADAS faults and speeds root-cause confirmation before a calibration job starts.

Mark says: If you run ADASLink, test this feature on your next ambiguous Bosch ADAS fault to shorten diagnostic guesswork.
