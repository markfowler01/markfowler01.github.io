Parts get pricey, ADAS gets organized
Monday, May 11 · #2

OEM parts pricing is climbing fast while the industry launches two major ADAS risk initiatives. Here's what matters to your shop this week.

INSURANCE

1. OEM parts prices jump as aftermarket stays flat

New OEM parts hit their largest price increase in Q1 2026, while recycled parts stay steady. This widens the gap between OEM and alternative parts sourcing. For shops already fighting insurers on supplement approvals, expect more pressure to spec aftermarket or recycled components.

Source: PartsTrader / SCRS →

TRAINING

2. Industry launches two ADAS risk initiatives

SCRS and the Collision Industry Conference just rolled out new programs to tackle ADAS opportunities and risks head-on. Details are thin, but both orgs are signaling that collision repairers need structured guidance on ADAS liability and calibration best practices.

Source: AutoBody News / SCRS →

INDUSTRY

3. LKQ hits record 40% alternative parts utilization

Aftermarket parts made up 40% of all parts used in Q1 2026—an all-time high. Insurers are betting hard on alternatives to cut claim costs. Shops need to stock up on competency with non-OEM parts sourcing and warranty handling.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

INDUSTRY

4. CCC touts AI momentum as insurers embrace platform

CCC Intelligent Solutions reported a strong Q1 2026, heavily boosted by AI adoption among insurer customers. The more insurers lean on CCC's AI tools, the more they'll automate estimate reviews and supplement denials. Shop profitability hinges on mastering CCC workflows.

Source: Repairer Driven News →

INDUSTRY

5. Driven Brands activist buyer offers 41% premium

ADW Capital Management bid $18 per share for Driven Brands (41% above market price)—signaling M&A heat in the collision consolidation space. If the deal closes, expect staffing and service changes across Driven's portfolio brands.

Source: Repairer Driven News →
