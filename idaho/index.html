How it works

Call or text the shop line with the car and the repair. We confirm a day, show up with the targets and scan tools that vehicle needs, and calibrate it in your bay. Treasure Valley shops get regular days. McCall and the Magic Valley are scheduled days, grouped so your cars get done without a trip to Boise.

What we calibrate

  • Forward-facing camera (static and dynamic)
  • Radar (front and rear)
  • Blind spot modules
  • 360-camera and surround-view systems
  • Parking-assist and ultrasonic sensors
  • Pre-scan and post-scan diagnostics with OEM-cited reports
