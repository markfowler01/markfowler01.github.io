Nobody touched the safety systems. The dash disagreed.
From the Van · Issue #3

A Grand Cherokee L came in for a quarter panel repair. Nothing near the front. Nothing near the radar.

Shop finished the work, went to hand it back, and the dash was a light show. Forward Collision Warning and Park Assist both throwing lamps.

Here's the part that makes people crazy. Nobody touched those systems. The body work was in the back.

But somewhere in the process the radio got a software update. On these Jeeps the infotainment module and the safety systems talk over the same network. A radio update that doesn't finish clean can leave the FCW and PEB modules confused about what version everybody else is running.

The fix wasn't a new module. The parts counter would have loved to sell one. The fix was completing the software flash correctly and then running the calibration the system was actually asking for.

Swap a module here and you've spent real money to fix nothing. The light comes right back.

The van has seen this exact dance on more than one Chrysler product. It is not surprised anymore.
