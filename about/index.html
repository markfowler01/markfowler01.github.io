Why this exists

Every body shop owner I work with is fighting the same battle: insurance carriers cutting calibration line items, denying procedures, forcing aftermarket parts. The shops winning are the ones with airtight documentation. The shops losing are the ones doing it by gut.

I built this stuff because the gap between those two is where shops die. The same OEM citations, the same scan reports, the same line-item discipline — repeatable. If a shop owner can grab a tool that flips a denial in 60 seconds, that's worth more than a year of conference talks.

"I don't write software for a living. I run a calibration shop. The tools you see here exist because they had to. If they help your shop get paid, they did their job."
