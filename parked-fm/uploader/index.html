PARKED FM Uploader

Internal tool. Used only by the PARKED FM channel owner.

What it does, once a day

  1. Creates an original lo-fi album and a car artwork, then renders the full video and a 30-second trailer.
  2. Uploads the full video to the PARKED FM channel with its title, description, tags and altered or synthetic content label (videos.insert).
  3. Sets the video's custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set).
  4. Uploads the trailer as a YouTube Short whose description links to the full video (videos.insert).

Authorization

Who signs in: only the channel owner, once, with Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen.

Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos and set thumbnails, nothing else).

Token storage: an encrypted GitHub Actions secret; never logged.

Revoke: Google Account > Security > Third-party access.

Data kept: none beyond the new video's ID, used to link the Short.
