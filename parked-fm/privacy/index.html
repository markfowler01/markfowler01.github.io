Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Who we are

PARKED FM is a YouTube channel run by Mark Fowler, owner of Absolute ADAS. The PARKED FM Uploader is a private software tool that publishes the channel's own videos. This policy covers the website at absoluteadas.com/parked-fm and the PARKED FM Uploader.

Use of YouTube API Services

The PARKED FM Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set thumbnails on the PARKED FM channel. By using YouTube content or features through this tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Who can use the Uploader

The Uploader is not offered to the public. It is authorized by one Google account only: the owner of the PARKED FM channel. It does not ask any other person to sign in and does not access any other person's YouTube account.

Information we access

  • An OAuth 2.0 authorization granted by the channel owner for the scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos and set their thumbnails) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly (read the channel's own totals: watch hours, and how many subscribers each of its videos brought in).
  • The ID of each video the Uploader creates, returned by YouTube after an upload, so the matching Short can link to it.

The Uploader does not read, collect, or analyze any other YouTube data: no information about individual viewers, no subscriber lists, no comments, and no other channels' content. Channel totals are used only to decide what kind of video to make next.

How we use it

Only to publish PARKED FM's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails to the PARKED FM channel, and to link each daily Short to its full video. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

How we store it

The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the GitHub repository that runs the Uploader. It is never written to logs or files. Video IDs appear only in that day's job log, which GitHub deletes automatically after its retention period (90 days at most).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information obtained through YouTube API Services with anyone. Video files and their details are sent only to YouTube, as the upload itself.

Cookies and website visitors

The PARKED FM web pages do not use cookies, sign-in, or tracking. The Uploader does not place cookies or similar technology on anyone's device.

Revoking access and deletion

The channel owner can revoke the Uploader's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, or on request, we delete the stored token within 7 days, and any stored API data within 30 days at the latest. Videos already published stay on YouTube until they are removed in YouTube Studio.

Children

PARKED FM videos are not made for kids, and the Uploader does not knowingly handle any information about children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions or deletion requests: info@absoluteadas.com.
