Why It Matters

Miscalibrated ADAS
Is Silent and Dangerous.

Your car's safety systems -the ones that catch you if you drift lanes, the emergency braking, the blind spot monitors -they're only as good as their calibration. After any impact or repair, those sensors need professional realignment. Driving without it might feel fine. Until it isn't.

💥
Collision repairs destroy calibration

Even minor fender benders shift sensor positioning. Your safety features won't engage properly until recalibration.

🪟
Windshield replacement requires recalibration

Forward-facing cameras sit behind the windshield. Replacement = recalibration required. Many shops skip it.

🚫
Generic tools can't do OEM calibration

Aftermarket scan tools can read codes. They can't perform the precision calibration modern vehicles demand.

🛡️
You assume the liability

If a miscalibrated safety system contributes to an accident, responsibility falls to you. Professional documentation protects you.
